do i need the app?

yes. the blorb app is what locks the apps you pick, using apple’s screen time. it’s free on the App Store and has nothing to buy in it. the app also works without a blorb, but then unlocking is a slow press instead of a walk to the fridge.

how much is shipping?

you’ll see the shipping cost before you pay, on the checkout page. nothing is charged until you confirm there.

is checkout safe?

yes. you pay on a secure checkout page run by our payment provider, so your card number never touches blorb.app and we never see it.

can i send it back?

send back an unused blorb within 30 days of it arriving for a full refund. if it arrives broken or doesn’t work, we’ll replace or refund it, postage included. the details are on the refunds page.

something else?

write to hello@blorb.app and a human will write back.
