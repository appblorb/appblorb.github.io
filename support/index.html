help

stuck, or something not right? write to hello@blorb.app and a person will answer.

report a name

if a name in a space is not all right, press that member's row on the board and choose "report this name", or write to the address above with the space's code. reports are looked at within a day. you can also hide a member from the same page.

common questions

  • my apps won't open. tap your blorb. if you really can't get to it, the home page has "can't get to my blorb?": a one-minute hold that works three times a year.
  • i don't have a blorb yet. the app works without one; opening up is a slow press.
  • how do i delete my account? in the app: settings, you, delete account.
  • how do i remove blorb's lock altogether? when nothing is locked, delete the app, or switch off its access in the settings app under screen time.
  • i can't delete apps. strict mode is on and a lock is running. tap your blorb (or wait for the lock to end), and deleting works again.
