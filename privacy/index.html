privacy

last updated: 17 november 2026. this covers the blorb app for iphone and the website blorb.app.

the short version

blorb is built to know as little about you as it can. there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, in the app or on the website, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

what stays on your phone

  • the apps and websites you lock. apple's screen time keeps the list of apps. blorb cannot read which apps they are, and nothing about them leaves your phone.
  • your schedules, your bursts and your time away. kept on your phone, and shared only with blorb's own widget and lock screen card.
  • your blorb. which one you have, its name and what it wears.

what goes to icloud, and only if you use spaces

spaces are optional. if you sign in and join one, blorb saves one row for you in each space, in apple's icloud (cloudkit). a row holds:

  • your blorb's name, its kind and what it is wearing;
  • how long you have been away from your phone today, and on each of the last two weeks' days;
  • an identifier apple gives blorb for your icloud account (not your name, email or phone number).

the name of a space is saved too. spaces are joined with a six-character code. they are kept in the app's public cloudkit database, which means the code is what keeps a space private: share it only with the people you want in it.

signing in

blorb uses sign in with apple, and asks apple for nothing but an identifier. it does not ask for your name or your email address.

nfc

blorb reads the tag inside a blorb to know it was tapped. the tag holds a link with the blorb's kind and serial code. nothing about you is written to it.

the website

  • the waitlist. if you give your email address on blorb.app, it is kept by kit (kit.com), the service that sends the email, and used only to tell you when blorbs are out. every email has a link to unsubscribe, and you can ask for your address to be deleted at any time.
  • cookies. blorb.app sets no cookies and has no trackers, so there is no cookie banner.
  • fonts and scripts. the home page loads its typeface from google fonts and one script from cdnjs. as with any web request, those services see your ip address.

why this is allowed, and how long it is kept

  • your row in a space is kept because you asked to be on that board (it is needed to give you the feature), until you leave the space or delete your account.
  • a waitlist address is kept because you agreed to hear from us, until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be removed.

your rights and deleting your data

  • in the app: settings, you, delete account. this removes your row from every space and forgets your sign-in.
  • start over (settings, about) also erases everything blorb keeps on the phone.
  • by email: write to hello@blorb.app to see, correct or delete anything held about you, or to complain. you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • to stop apple signing you in to blorb, open the settings app, your name, sign in with apple, blorb, and choose to stop using it.

children

blorb is not directed at children under 13 (or the age your country sets, up to 16), and does not knowingly collect information from them. if you think a child has given us information, write to us and it will be deleted.

changes and contact

if this changes, the date above changes with it. questions: hello@blorb.app.
