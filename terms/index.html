what blorb is

blorb is a small app that locks the apps you choose until you get up and tap your blorb. by using it you agree to these terms.

using blorb

  • blorb works through apple's screen time. it is a help with your own habits, not a safety or parental-control product, and it is not medical advice.
  • the app is free and works without a blorb: opening up is then a slow press instead of a tap.
  • you can always switch blorb's access off in the settings app under screen time. if you turn on strict mode, apps cannot be deleted from your phone while a lock is on; that is your choice and you can turn it off whenever nothing is locked.

spaces and names

  • the names you give your blorb and your spaces are seen by the other people in a space. keep them friendly: nothing abusive, hateful, sexual or threatening.
  • you can report a name from a member's page in the app, or hide that member. reports are looked at within a day, and a name that breaks these terms is removed, along with the account behind it if needed.
