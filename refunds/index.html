refunds

last updated: 17 november 2026. for blorbs bought on blorb.app.

changed your mind

you can send back an unused blorb within 30 days of it arriving, for a full refund of what you paid for it. write to hello@blorb.app first and we'll tell you where to send it. return postage is yours to pay unless the blorb is faulty.

something wrong with it

if a blorb arrives broken, or its tag doesn't work, write to hello@blorb.app with your order number and a photo. we'll replace it or refund it, postage included.

how refunds are paid

to the card or account you paid with, within 14 days of the blorb getting back to us.

the app

the blorb app is free and has nothing to buy in it, so there is nothing to refund there.
